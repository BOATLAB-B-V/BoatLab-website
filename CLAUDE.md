# Working on the BoatLab website

- Read `PLAN.md` at the start of a session; update its checklist and session log before finishing.
- `README.md` describes the pages, project layout and build.
- Edit sources in `boatlab-site/src/` only; `dist/`, `inline/` and `tilda/` are generated (`cd boatlab-site && python3 build.py`) and not in git. `boatlab-site/assets/` is also generated by the build but IS committed: a CDN serves it, with `img/`, `video/` and `fonts/`, to the pages pasted into Tilda. Always rebuild before pushing so it is current.
- Tilda rule: the existing Tilda pages (marketing quiz pages, landing pages used by ads) are never edited, renamed or deleted. New pages are added next to them; the old home page is renamed, not removed, before the new one becomes the main page.
- Hosting: the pages live in Tilda as T123 HTML blocks (`tilda/*.html`, checklist in `tilda/PAGES.md`); see the go-live runbook in `PLAN.md`.
- All visible text lives in `boatlab-site/src/assets/site.js` and must stay in step in English, Dutch and German. `build.py` writes every page per language (`/`, `/nl/`, `/de/`); page titles and descriptions are in `build.py` (META) and `SP` in site.js.
- Style: white content pages with deep-navy bands (hero, crane, teaser, CTA, footer); logo blue `#0b8fd6` as accent; photos and videos in rounded frames, no edge fades. At most one video per screen.
- Photos in `boatlab-site/img/` must be web-ready (resized and compressed). Keep large originals out of the repo.
- The owner is not a developer: explain changes in plain language, and push to GitHub only when asked.
