Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data BoatLab collects through boatlabservice.nl, why we use it, who works with it, how long we keep it and what your rights are. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

Boatlab BV
Geijsterseweg 10, 5861 BL Wanssum, the Netherlands
Email: info@boatlabservice.nl
Phone: +31 6 11742272

2. Which data we collect

When you send a service request through the form on our contact page: your name, email address, phone number (optional), preferred language, boat make and model, boat length, where the boat is located, the services you are interested in, your preferred date, any message you add, and the page you sent it from. You can select photos in the form, but they are not sent with it: if you want to share photos, we ask you to email them to us.

When you use one of our campaign pages (for example the antifouling price calculator): your answers to the questions (such as boat make, length and the work you want), your name, phone number and email address, and the campaign the visit came from (for example which ad you clicked).

When you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp or social media: the details you share with us in that conversation.

When you visit the website: our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, to deliver the website and keep it secure.

3. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • To answer your request, prepare a quote and plan and carry out the work, including the crane lift, berth or storage. Legal basis: steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep our administration and invoices. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • To keep the website secure and working, and to see which of our campaigns bring in requests (using the campaign codes in the page address, not cookies). Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To measure our advertising with cookies (see section 8). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. How we handle your request

A request from the website or a campaign page is stored in our own request system, where our team follows it up. A copy goes to a Google Sheet that we use as an overview and backup, and our team receives a short notification with the details of the request in an internal Telegram group, so we can reply quickly. Emails and photos you send us arrive in our Microsoft 365 mailbox. Only BoatLab staff, all based in the Netherlands, use this data for their work.

5. Who receives your data

We use service providers that process data on our behalf. They may only use it to provide their service to us:

  • our web hosting provider, which runs the website and our request system;
  • our marketing partner, who builds and maintains our campaign pages and the request system;
  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365) for email;
  • Google (Google Sheets) for the overview of requests;
  • Telegram for the internal notifications to our team.

Focus Marine Center in Wanssum, our partner at the yard, receives your name, phone number, boat details and the planned dates when your job involves their crane, a berth or storage, so they can plan this.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, that platform also processes your messages under its own privacy policy.

6. Data outside the European Economic Area

Microsoft, Google and Meta may process data in the United States. They are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection, or use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Telegram may process data outside the EEA as well; we only send the details of the request that our team needs to respond.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Service requests that do not lead to an order: up to 12 months after our last contact.
  • Customer data, quotes and invoices: 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

On the pages of this website we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts, photos and videos are loaded from our own server, so no data goes to Google Fonts or video platforms when you visit. The pages store nothing in your browser.

On our campaign pages (such as the antifouling price calculator) we use the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram) to measure which ads lead to requests. The pixel places cookies (for example _fbp, kept for up to 90 days) and sends technical data such as your IP address and the pages you visit to Meta. This requires your consent. We are adjusting these pages so that the pixel only loads after you agree.

If we start measuring visits on this website (for example with Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager), you will first see a cookie banner, and nothing is loaded until you click "Accept". Your choice is then saved in your browser (boatlab-consent), so we do not ask again. You can change it at any time through "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page, and we will update this policy before we start.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, to object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email us at info@boatlabservice.nl; we reply within one month.

You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or with the data protection authority in your own country.

10. Security

The website and the request form use an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and only the people who need your data for their work can access it.

11. Links to other websites

Our website links to Google Maps, WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok. These are not embedded on our pages: nothing is loaded from them until you click. Once you visit them, their own privacy policies apply.

12. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we start using a new tool. The date at the top shows the latest version. See also our terms of use.

  • onze hostingpartij, die de website en ons aanvraagsysteem draait;

  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365) voor e-mail;
